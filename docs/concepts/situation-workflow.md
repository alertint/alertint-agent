# Situation workflow

The [interactive Situation workflow](https://alertint.com/docs/concepts/situation-workflow)
shows how a Situation moves through its lifecycle, how investigation and evidence
feed its Finding, and what operators see in Slack. Select a state or transition
for its guard and next step; use **Slack ↗** to see an illustrative message.

The Finding remains visible throughout. Source clearance and the recovery grace
period determine recovery; an investigation or operator decision cannot declare
an alert recovered.
